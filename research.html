Know which result you are reading

The browser demonstration uses hand-written exact backends; it does not load these trained models. Recorded results are scoped to their datasets, controls and budgets. The ARC notebooks are drafts, the latest Go collection stopped below its required row count, and ongoing studies have no final outcome yet.

Reproduction instructions

Public source and evidence snapshot. Original hashes and notebook transformations are recorded in the repository. No general reasoning, clinical efficacy, competitive Go rank or official ARC score is claimed.
